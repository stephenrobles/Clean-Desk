## Version Information (1.0)

**Promotional text (170):**
Hide every file, folder and disk on your desktop with one click or a keyboard shortcut, so only the wallpaper shows. Nothing is moved. Bring it back any time.

**Description (4000):**
Clean Desk clears your Mac desktop in one click. Choose Hide Desktop Icons from the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥⌘D from any app, and every file, folder, mounted drive and disk image on the desktop is out of sight: only the wallpaper shows. Click again and everything is back exactly where it was.

It's made for the moments when the desktop shouldn't be the star: sharing your screen, recording a video, giving a presentation, or just clearing your head for an hour of focused work.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click the icon in the menu bar and choose Hide Desktop Icons, or press ⌃⌥⌘D anywhere. Record a different shortcut in Settings if you prefer
• While hidden, a clean backdrop covers the desktop icons on every display. Your files are untouched, and anything you save or drag to the desktop in the meantime is waiting there when you show it again
• The menu bar icon turns into a sparkle while the desktop is hidden, so you always know where things stand
• Choose Show Desktop Icons, or press the shortcut again, and the backdrop is gone

A WALLPAPER JUST FOR HIDING
• Pick any image and Clean Desk shows it on every display while the desktop is hidden. A plain backdrop for recordings and calls, or something calmer than your everyday wallpaper
• Without one, Clean Desk uses your current wallpaper when it can, and a plain blue backdrop otherwise

SET IT AND FORGET IT
• Launch at login, so Clean Desk is in the menu bar from the moment your Mac starts
• Keep it as a menu bar item only, or show it in the Dock too
• Open cleandesk://hide, cleandesk://show or cleandesk://toggle from Shortcuts, a launcher or a script to control the desktop from your own automations

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Clean Desk never reads, lists, moves or copies the files on your desktop
• No account, no analytics, no tracking, and no network access of any kind

Clean Desk requires macOS 15 or later.

## App Review Information

- **Sign-in required:** No
- **Contact:** Stephen Robles, steve@beard.fm
- **Notes:**
  Clean Desk is a menu bar utility (no Dock icon by default). After launching, click the grid icon in the menu
  bar and choose "Hide Desktop Icons", or press Control-Option-Command-D. A window at the desktop level then
  covers the desktop icons on every display, drawing the current wallpaper (or an image chosen in Settings),
  so the desktop appears empty; ordinary windows, the Dock and the menu bar are unaffected. The menu bar icon
  becomes a sparkle while hidden. Choose "Show Desktop Icons" or press the shortcut again to remove the cover.
  Settings (Command-comma, or Settings… in the menu) has the shortcut recorder, the custom wallpaper option
  (chosen through a standard Open panel), launch at login and the Dock icon switch. The app makes no network
  requests and collects no data.
