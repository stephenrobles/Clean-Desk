Clean Desk 1.0

  • Hide every file, folder and disk on the desktop from the menu bar, or with ⌃⌥⌘D from any app. Change the shortcut in Settings.
  • Optional custom wallpaper while the desktop is hidden; your usual wallpaper comes back when icons return.
  • Launch at login, an optional Dock icon, and cleandesk://hide, show and toggle URLs for Shortcuts and launchers.
